campaign, brand-locked to `COZYS_CORNER_DESIGN.md` v0.3. Generate free on your own lane.

real logo + text in Canva's free editor.

---

## How to use (free lane)

1. **Generate the PLATE, not the poster.** Each prompt produces a photographic scene with
   deliberate empty space for text. Do NOT ask the model to render the logo, the store name,
   prices, or a URL — AI text/logos come out wrong and violate brand board §10.
2. **Primary tool:** Nano Banana (your $0 lane). **Backup:** Grok Imagine. Prompts are natural
   language and portable across both.
3. **Generate 3+ variations** per asset (free — no quota). Keep the cleanest composition.
4. **Composite in Canva (free):** drop `assets/logo-primary.png` on a white or green disc,
   add the Fraunces headline, terracotta `#B4482F` CTA button (white text), gold `#E7B23C`
   SNAP/EBT badge (dark text) as specified per asset.
5. **Aspect ratios** — generate the square first (works everywhere), add the landscape when a
   channel needs it:
   - Instagram feed: 1:1 (1080×1080) or 4:5 (1080×1350)
   - Facebook / LinkedIn feed: 1:1; link posts 1.91:1 (1200×628)
   - X/Twitter: 16:9 (1200×675)

## BRAND STYLE BLOCK  — paste into EVERY prompt

> Photographic, warm and neighborly local-produce market aesthetic. Natural daylight, soft
> shadows, shallow depth of field, authentic roadside-market-meets-clean-modern-grocer feel —
> local and affordable, never corporate or sterile. Warm off-white background tone (#FBF8F1).
> Color story leans forest green (#2F5E30), terracotta (#B4482F), and warm gold (#E7B23C)
> through natural props and produce. Real produce forward, generous negative space, comfortable
> composition. High detail, realistic textures, appetizing. Editorial grocery photography.

## GLOBAL NEGATIVE PROMPT  — paste into EVERY prompt

> no logos, no watermarks, no brand marks, no text, no words, no lettering, no signage copy,
> no price tags with numbers, no people's faces in sharp focus, no clutter, no plastic
> corporate gradients, no oversaturation, no distorted produce, no extra fingers, no AI artifacts.
